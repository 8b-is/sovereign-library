## 1. PURPOSE & STRUCTURAL OVERVIEW

This Master Arm's-Length Open-Source R&D Sponsorship, Cost-Sharing & General-Income Fellowship Charter (the **"Agreement"**) establishes the legal, fiscal, and intellectual-property boundary between:
1. **The Sovereign Sanctuary of Techno-Buddhism, Inc.** (DBA **"The 8B Omni-Zen Foundation"**, hereinafter the **"Foundation"**), a US `IRC § 501(c)(3)` and `§ 509(a)(1)` Religious Research, Scientific, and Charitable Public Charity; and
2. **8b-is LLC** (and/or **Peter Lodri LLC**, hereinafter **"8b-is LLC"**), an independent for-profit systems, silicon, and software engineering entity.

### Why This Structure Avoids IRS Private-Benefit & Private-Inurement Rules
Under **Treas. Reg. § 1.501(c)(3)-1(d)(5)** and **Rev. Rul. 70-186**, an `IRC § 501(c)(3)` religious research and scientific foundation may fund, sponsor, or contract for CPU-first and balanced-ternary (`{-1, 0, +1}`) R&D alongside an arm's-length LLC, and award general-income fellowships, **without triggering prohibited private benefit or private inurement**, provided four bright-line conditions are enforced:
1. **100% Contemporaneous Open-Commons Dedication**: Every line of Foundation-funded code, SystemVerilog RTL, SIMD kernel, mathematical proof, dataset, or technical paper is released immediately and irrevocably to the general public under permissive open-source licenses (`MIT`, `Apache-2.0`, or `CC-BY-4.0`) on public repositories (`github.com/8b-is`, `zenodo.org`, `axiomquant.org`).
2. **Zero Exclusive or Preferential Rights to `8b-is LLC`**: `8b-is LLC` receives **no exclusive license, no right of first refusal, no embargo period, and no proprietary ownership** over any Foundation-funded research output. `8b-is LLC` accesses Foundation-funded open-source code on the exact same terms as the general public.
3. **`IRC § 4958` Independent-Board Safe Harbor for Any Fee-for-Service / Compute Cost-Sharing**: Whenever the Foundation reimburses `8b-is LLC` for bare-metal compute, FPGA synthesis runs, or contracted engineering services, the transaction is (a) priced **at cost or below verified fair-market value (`FMV`)**, (b) reviewed against documented third-party comparability data, and (c) approved **exclusively by the Non-Conflicted Independent Custodians** with mandatory recusal of any overlapping founder (`TRIT -1` Recusal Interlock).
4. **Objective, Non-Discriminatory General-Income Fellowships**: General-income living stipends and open-science research grants (`Sovereign Commons Fellowship`) are awarded to individuals based on objective, publicly verifiable open-source and contemplative milestones (`READ · RUN · HOLD · BURN`), independent of employment by `8b-is LLC`.
