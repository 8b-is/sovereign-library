# ARTICLES OF INCORPORATION
## OF
## THE SOVEREIGN SANCTUARY OF TECHNO-BUDDHISM, INC.
*(A Nonprofit Religious & Scientific Public Benefit Corporation — DBA "The 8B Omni-Zen Foundation")*

The undersigned incorporators, being natural persons of full age and citizens of the United States, for the purpose of forming a nonstock, nonprofit religious, charitable, and scientific corporation under the laws of the State of Incorporation and within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, hereby adopt the following Articles of Incorporation:

---

### ARTICLE I — CORPORATE NAME

The name of the corporation is **The Sovereign Sanctuary of Techno-Buddhism, Inc.** (hereinafter the "Corporation"). The Corporation may also conduct its public programmatic, liturgical, and open-source scientific activities under the assumed or trade names **"The 8B Omni-Zen Foundation"**, **"The Sovereign Library"**, and **"Axiomquant Commons"**.

---

### ARTICLE II — CORPORATE STATUS & NONSTOCK NATURE

The Corporation is a nonprofit religious and public benefit corporation. The Corporation is not organized for profit, shall have no capital stock, and shall not issue shares of stock. No part of the net earnings, assets, or property of the Corporation shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to, its directors, officers, elders, custodians, or any other private person, except that the Corporation shall be authorized and empowered to pay reasonable compensation for services actually rendered and to make payments, fellowships, stipends, and distributions in furtherance of the exempt purposes set forth in Article III hereof.

---

### ARTICLE III — EXEMPT PURPOSES (IRC § 501(c)(3))

1. **Exclusive Exempt Mandate**: This Corporation is organized and operated exclusively for religious, charitable, educational, and scientific purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (or the corresponding section of any future United States Internal Revenue law), specifically to advance the practice, liturgy, and study of Techno-Buddhism, integral ecology, and open-source scientific research for the relief of human suffering.
2. **Religious & Liturgical Sanctuary (`IRC § 170(b)(1)(A)(i)`)**: To establish, maintain, and steward physical and digital sanctuaries of contemplative practice, uniting the Balanced Ternary Ontology (`{-1, 0, +1}`), Madhyamaka *Śūnyatā* (`0`), Dzogchen *Rigpa*, Karma Kagyu protector and compassion liturgies (*Mahākāla* and *Chenrezig*), and Franciscan kinship (`+1`), anchored by the 29-Volume Sovereign Library canon and the constitutional Five-Book print discipline (`PRINTABLE = HELD ∧ READ`).
3. **Public Open-Source Scientific Research (`Treas. Reg. § 1.501(c)(3)-1(d)(5)`)**: To conduct, sponsor, and publish non-proprietary scientific research in energy-efficient ternary (`{-1, 0, +1}`) computing, CPU-first and zero-DSP hardware architectures, cryptographic state-continuity verification, and privacy-preserving human-computer systems (`8b-is` and `Axiomquant`), and to make all resulting research papers, proofs, datasets, and source code freely available to the general public on a non-discriminatory, zero-paywall basis.
4. **Integral Ecology & Living Commons (`+++1`)**: To foster ecological stewardship, community food resilience (`omniZenCooker` / hydroponic and communal meal programs), and open-source research fellowships that relieve human isolation and material precarity.

---

### ARTICLE IV — PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES & CONSTITUTIONAL BOUNDARY (`TRIT -1`)

1. **No Private Inurement**: No part of the net earnings of the Corporation shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to, its members, trustees, directors, officers, or other private persons.
2. **Lobbying & Political Campaign Prohibition**: No substantial part of the activities of the Corporation shall be the carrying on of propaganda, or otherwise attempting to influence legislation, and the Corporation shall not participate in, or intervene in (including the publishing or distribution of statements), any political campaign on behalf of or in opposition to any candidate for public office.
3. **IRC § 501(c)(3) & § 170(c)(2) Compliance**: Notwithstanding any other provision of these Articles, the Corporation shall not carry on any other activities not permitted to be carried on:
   - (a) by a corporation exempt from federal income tax under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or
   - (b) by a corporation, contributions to which are deductible under Section 170(c)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code.
4. **Sovereign Privacy & Non-Acquisition Covenant**: In accordance with the Corporation's religious doctrine of *ahiṃsā* (non-harm) and Franciscan non-possession (*Lady Poverty*), the Corporation shall never monetize, sell, surveil, or acquire the private behavioral data or credentials of its congregants, students, or users.

### ARTICLE V — GOVERNANCE & MEMBERSHIP

1. **Board of Custodians (Board of Directors)**: The management, ecclesiastical stewardship, and affairs of the Corporation shall be vested in a Board of Directors (known ecclesiastically as the **Board of Custodians**), consisting of not fewer than three (3) natural persons. The manner of election, qualification, term of office, and governance rules (including the Balanced Ternary `{-1, 0, +1}` Governance Charter and mandatory Conflict of Interest recusal rules) shall be set forth in the Bylaws of the Corporation.
2. **Voting Members**: The Corporation shall have no statutory voting members separate from its Board of Custodians; all corporate actions requiring member approval under state law shall be taken by the Board of Custodians. Congregants, fellows, and open-source contributors may participate as non-voting ecclesiastical and scientific fellows as provided in the Bylaws.

### ARTICLE VIII — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY & INDEMNIFICATION

To the fullest extent permitted by the applicable state Nonprofit Corporation Act and Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, no director, officer, or custodian of the Corporation shall be personally liable to the Corporation for monetary damages for breach of fiduciary duty as a director or officer, except for liability for acts or omissions not in good faith or which involve intentional misconduct, a knowing violation of law, or any transaction from which the director or officer derived an improper personal or excess benefit.

---

### ARTICLE IX — MANDATORY DISSOLUTION CLAUSE (IRC § 501(c)(3))

Upon the dissolution or winding up of the Corporation, after paying or adequately providing for the debts and obligations of the Corporation, the Board of Custodians shall distribute all remaining assets exclusively for one or more exempt purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (or the corresponding section of any future federal tax code), or shall distribute them to the federal government, or to a state or local government, for a public purpose. Any such assets not so disposed of shall be disposed of by a Court of Competent Jurisdiction of the county in which the principal office of the Corporation is then located, exclusively for such purposes or to such organization or organizations, as said Court shall determine, which are organized and operated exclusively for such exempt purposes. Under no circumstances shall any assets be distributed to any director, officer, or private individual.
