## ARTICLE V — CONFLICT OF INTEREST & ARM'S-LENGTH TRANSACTION POLICY
*(Compliant with IRS Form 1023 Appendix A & IRC § 4958)*

### Section 5.1. Purpose
The purpose of this Conflict of Interest Policy is to protect the tax-exempt interest of **The Sovereign Sanctuary of Techno-Buddhism, Inc.** when it is contemplating entering into a transaction, grant, or arrangement that might benefit the private interest of an officer, director, or related entity (specifically including, without limitation, **Peter Lodri LLC**).

### Section 5.2. Duty to Disclose & Mandatory Recusal
1. **Disclosure**: In connection with any actual or possible conflict of interest, an interested Director or Officer must disclose the existence of the financial interest and be given the opportunity to disclose all material facts to the non-conflicted Directors.
2. **Mandatory Recusal**: Any Director who has a direct or indirect financial interest in a proposed contract, grant, or vendor relationship (including any Master Services Agreement or hardware R&D contract with **Peter Lodri LLC**) **shall leave the room/vote and recuse themselves** from both the final deliberation and the vote on that transaction.
3. **Arm's-Length Fair Market Value Standard**: The non-conflicted Directors may approve such a transaction only if they determine in good faith, based on appropriate comparability data documented in the minutes, that:
   - (a) the transaction is in the Corporation's best interest and for its own exempt benefit;
   - (b) the transaction is fair and reasonable to the Corporation (at or below fair-market value); and
   - (c) all software, documentation, or research funded by the Corporation under the arrangement is released open-source to the public commons.
