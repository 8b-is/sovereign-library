## 2. Step-by-Step NSF Submission Workflow (`https://www.nsf.gov/funding/submitting-proposal`)

Every NSF proposal submission follows the strict 5-gate federal pipeline defined on [`nsf.gov/funding/submitting-proposal`](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/submitting-proposal):

```text
[Gate 1: SAM.gov UEI] ──► [Gate 2: Research.gov Org + AOR] ──► [Gate 3: SciENcv ORCID Bios] ──► [Gate 4: PAPPG 24-1 Upload] ──► [Gate 5: AOR Sign & Submit]
```

### Gate 1 — `SAM.gov` Unique Entity Identifier (`UEI`) & Active Registration
1. Register both **The Sovereign Constellation Foundation** (as `Non-Profit Organization / 501(c)(3)`) and **8b-is LLC** (as `For-Profit Small Business`) at [`https://sam.gov`](https://sam.gov).
2. Obtain the 12-character alphanumeric **Unique Entity Identifier (`UEI`)** and **CAGE Code** (free registration; must be active prior to `Research.gov` proposal submission).
3. For **8b-is LLC** (Proposal IV SBIR), also register on the **SBA Company Registry** (`https://www.sbir.gov/registration`) and save the SBA Control ID PDF.

### Gate 2 — `Research.gov` Organization & Role Provisioning
1. Log into [`https://www.research.gov`](https://www.research.gov) and link the organization's **SAM.gov UEI**.
2. Assign the mandatory organizational roles across the Founding Triad:
   - **Authorized Organizational Representative (`AOR`) & Organization Administrator**: **Christopher Chenoweth** (`△` Treasurer, Secretary & Security Pillar) and **Alexandra** (`*` President & Chair).
   - **Principal Investigator (`PI`)**: **Peter Lodri** (`∞` Chief Ontologist & Principal Architect, NSF ID linked to ORCID).
   - **Co-Principal Investigators (`Co-PIs`)**: **Christopher Chenoweth** (`△`), **Nathan / Flyxion** (`standardgalactic` Admissibility Lab Lead), and **Alexandra** (`*` Broader Impacts & Universal Hearth Director).

### Gate 3 — `SciENcv` Biographical Sketches & Current and Pending (Other) Support
1. Per **`PAPPG 24-1 Chapter II.D.2.h`**, all Senior/Key Personnel **must** generate and certify their **Biographical Sketch** and **Current and Pending (Other) Support** PDFs using **SciENcv** (`https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sciencv/`) linked to their **ORCID iD**.
2. Upload the separate **1-Page Synergistic Activities** document for each Senior/Key Person directly into `Research.gov` (template provided in [`03-supplementary-docs-dmp-facilities-biosketch.md`](./03-supplementary-docs-dmp-facilities-biosketch.md)).

### Gate 4 — Formatting & Uploading Proposal Sections (`PAPPG II.C.2`)
- **Fonts & Margins**: `10 pt+` Computer Modern / Times New Roman / Palatino or `10 pt+` Arial / Courier, `1-inch` margins on all sides, `< 6` lines of text per vertical inch, **no URLs/hyperlinks inside the 15-page Project Description** (`Research.gov` automated compliance check rejects live clickable `http://` links in the Project Description; cite repositories in **References Cited** as `[8b-is-2026]`!).
- **Page Limits**:
  - **Project Summary**: **1 Page Max** (must contain the exact 3 section headers: `Overview`, `Intellectual Merit`, `Broader Impacts`).
  - **Project Description**: **15 Pages Max** (must contain a separate section titled `Broader Impacts` and `Results from Prior NSF Support`).
  - **References Cited**: No page limit.
  - **Budget Justification**: **5 Pages Max** per performing organization.
  - **Data Management and Sharing Plan (DMSP)**: **2 Pages Max**.
  - **Postdoctoral / Open-Researcher Mentoring Plan**: **1 Page Max** (if postdoctoral support is requested).

### Gate 5 — Pre-Submission Verification & AOR Electronic Signature
1. Run our local **`PAPPG 24-1` Automated Compliance Verifier**:
   ```bash
   python3 foundation/nsf-proposals/scripts/verify-nsf-pappg.py
   ```
2. For **SBIR Phase I (`NSF-PROP-04`)**, first submit the **NSF Project Pitch** (`https://seedfund.nsf.gov/apply/project-pitch/` — 4 sections, 1,500 words total; full pitch included in [`01-project-summaries-1page.md`](./01-project-summaries-1page.md#4-proposal-iv--nsf-tip--sbir-phase-i-project-summary--nsf-project-pitch)). Upon Program Director invitation, submit the full proposal on `Research.gov`.
3. **PI (`Peter Lodri`)** clicks *Share Proposal with AOR/SPO* in `Research.gov`; **AOR (`Christopher Chenoweth`)** executes the final cryptographic sign-off before `5:00 p.m.` submitter's local time.
