## 3. AT-COST PRICING & ZERO-PROFIT COVENANT (`IRC § 4958 SAFE HARBOR`)

### 3.1. Strict At-Cost Reimbursement (`0% Profit Markup`)
To eliminate any possibility of excess benefit or private inurement under IRC `§ 4958`, **8b-is** agrees that all services, compute hosting, and hardware prototyping provided to the Foundation under this Agreement shall be billed **strictly at documented actual direct cost with zero percent (0%) profit margin**, or donated in-kind at $0 charge.

### 3.2. Pre-Approval & Comparability Data
Prior to executing any Task Order requiring a payment from the Foundation:
1. The **Independent R&D, Audit & Compensation Committee** shall review independent market comparability data confirming that the proposed cost reimbursement is substantially below commercial market rates for equivalent systems engineering;
2. **Alexandra**, **Christopher**, and **Peter Lodri** shall recuse themselves from both deliberation and voting; and
3. No payment shall be disbursed until the deliverables and their public open-source release receipts (`SHA-256` / `ZKP1` digests and public repository URLs) are verified by an Independent Director.

## 4. 100% OPEN-SOURCE PUBLIC DEDICATION (`26 CFR § 1.501(c)(3)-1(d)(5)`)

### 4.1. Immediate Public Release
In strict compliance with Treasury Regulation `§ 1.501(c)(3)-1(d)(5)(iii)` and `Rev. Rul. 76-296`, **all research results, source code, ternary kernels, mathematical proofs, datasets, hardware schematics, and manuscripts funded in whole or in part by the Foundation shall be published freely and immediately to the general public** upon completion of each milestone, without paywalls, delays, or commercial embargoes.

### 4.2. Irrevocable Open Licensing & No Exclusive Rights for `8b-is`
All software and hardware designs produced under this Agreement shall be licensed irrevocably to the public under the **MIT License** or **Apache License 2.0**, and all scholarly, literary, and liturgical texts under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (`CC-BY-4.0`)** or Public Domain Dedication (`CC0`). **8b-is LLC** shall never acquire any exclusive right, patent monopoly, closed-source derivative right, or commercial priority over any Foundation-funded work.

## 5. SEPARATION OF OPERATIONS, FINANCES & DATA

1. **Financial Separation:** The Foundation and **8b-is** shall maintain completely separate bank accounts, accounting ledgers, tax filings, and corporate records.
2. **Publication Projection Data Firewall:** Neither party shall transmit raw personal data ($S$) of donors, grant applicants, or sanctuary participants to the other; any shared telemetry must satisfy the Foundation's **Non-Acquisition** and **Non-Retention** invariants (`I(Y; S) -> min`).
3. **Termination at Will:** The Foundation's Independent Committee may terminate this Agreement or any Task Order at any time, with or without cause, upon seven (7) days' written notice without penalty.
