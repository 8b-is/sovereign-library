### Section 5.2. Mandatory Recusal & Three-Step Safe Harbor (`26 CFR § 53.4958-6`)
Whenever a transaction involves **8b-is LLC** or any member of the Founding Family:
1. **Full Disclosure & Recusal:** Alexandra, Christopher, and Peter shall disclose all material facts regarding their interest and shall leave the room (or virtual vote channel) during deliberation and voting.

3. **Contemporaneous Written Documentation:** The Committee shall record the basis for its determination in the corporate minutes prior to the execution of the contract.

Every line of code, ternary kernel, hardware schematic, mathematical paper, or manuscript funded in whole or in part by the Foundation shall be published freely to the public under an irrevocable open-source or open-access license (`MIT`, `Apache-2.0`, or `CC-BY-4.0`) upon completion.
