  Under Treasury Regulation `§ 1.501(c)(3)-1(d)(5)`, the Foundation conducts and sponsors fundamental and applied scientific research in the public interest focused on **accessible, ultra-low-power computing architectures that do not require expensive, energy-intensive corporate GPU clusters**.
