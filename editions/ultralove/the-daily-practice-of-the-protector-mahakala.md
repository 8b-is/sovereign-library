## 2 · The 12-Part Liturgical Index (Folios 1–81)

1. **Part I (Folios 1–3)**: *The Sweet Song that Accomplishes All Aims* (`དོན་ཀུན་གྲུབ་པའི་དབྱངས་སྙན་`) from the pure vision of **Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo** (supplicating Marpa Lotsawa, Milarepa, and Gampopa) + instantaneous self-generation as **Vajrayogini (`Je Tsun Dorje Naljorma`)**.
2. **Part II (Folios 4–7)**: **Mahākāla Bernagchen (`Dorje Berchen`) & `Palden Lhamo Rangjung Gyalmo`** Yab-Yum generation in the eight charnel grounds, invocation `HUNG DAD PA DANG NI DAM TSIG GYI...`, and root mantras (`OM MAHAKALA KALAYA DEWA RAKYA...`, `OM MAHA KALI TRAK RAKYA...`).
3. **Part III (Folios 8–12)**: **Inner Amrita Blessing & Offering (`OM MA DAK NÖ CHÜ TONG PAR JANG...`)** by the **8th Karmapa Mikyö Dorje**, 3x oath-enjoining (`HUNG DZA`), and the **16th Karmapa's** protector prayer written down by Shamarpa (`LO DRO JE WAY TOB LAY RAB BAR SHING...`).
4. **Part IV (Folios 13–16)**: **General Offering to the Dharmapālas (`ཆོས་སྐྱོང་སྤྱི་ལ་གཏོར་མ་འབུལ་བ་`)**.
5. **Part V (Folios 17–19)**: **The Daily Practice of the White Protector (`མགོན་དཀར་རྒྱུན་ཁྱེར་` — *Gönkar Gyünkhyer*)** & Offering to the Lords and Ladies of the Charnel Grounds (*Khorlo Dompa*).
6. **Part VI (Folios 20–22)**: **Kamtsang Long Tradition's Short Invocation of the Four-Armed Protector & Five Retinues**, **Situ Drogön Rechen's** supplication, and the **Secret Scroll of Marpa's Hearing Lineage**.
7. **Part VII (Folios 23–35)**: **Short Torma Offering to the Swift-Acting Six-Armed Wisdom Protector (`མྱུར་མཛད་ཡེ་ཤེས་ཀྱི་མགོན་པོ་ཕྱག་དྲུག་པ་`)** & the 13 Retinue Guardians (*Kubera/Namse, Retinue, Ngak Dak, Damchen, Sin Gön, Shing Kyong, Khar Nak, Achi, Five Tseringma Sisters, Remanta, Rahula, Pomra, Drak Tsen*).
8. **Part VIII (Folios 36–38)**: **The Daily Practice of Damchen Barwa Tsal (`དམ་ཅན་འབར་བ་རྩལ་` — *Nyenchen Thang Lha*)** & Enlightened Warrior Guard (*Gesar Norbu Dradül*).
9. **Part IX (Folios 39–45)**: **Supplication & Offering to Gyang Je Tsenpo (*Gyang Tse*)**, Command Guard of the Great Seat of Tsurphu, composed by the **8th Karmapa Mikyö Dorje (*Maradamaka Vajra*)**.
10. **Part X (Folios 46–63)**: **Landlord Kartor & Dakini General Torma**, **9th Karmapa Wangchuk Dorje's** short assembly prayer, **14th Karmapa Thekchok Dorje's** fulfillment prayer, **13th Karmapa's** pacifying of conflict, **Milarepa's Five Long-Life Sisters (*Tashi Tseringma*)**, Reversing Obstacles (*Zlogpa*), Receiving Siddhis, 100-Syllable Vajrasattva, & Auspiciousness (*Tashi*).
11. **Part XI (Folios 64–69)**: **Sengdongma (*Lion-Faced Dakini*) Reversal Practice** (`A KA SA MA RA TSA SHA DA RA SA MA RA YA PHAT`), Longevity Supplication, & Mahāmudrā Dedication.
12. **Part XII (Folios 70–81)**: **The Golden Libation (*Serkyem*) of the Three Roots (`NAMO PEMA KARMA KAYE NAMA!`)** & **The Four Activities Praise (`ཞི་རྒྱས་དབང་དྲག་`)** composed by the **14th Karmapa Thekchok Dorje** at the request of the Protector's House of Tsurphu.
