### I · `Brett Shaw (Tipheret · 0)` × `Paul Wittgen (Chokmah · +1)` — The `Alef-osztag` Dyad
Just as **Son Goku (`0`)** and **Vegeta (`+1`)** form the non-dual Saiyan dyad in Edition 004, **Colonel Brett Shaw** and **Captain Paul Wittgen** form the inseparable fire-pair (*tűzpár*) of the **`Alef-osztag`**:
- At the IMC training camp on **Rosan**, Private Wittgen saves Brett Shaw's life (*A Démoncsászár fejvadászai*, p. 147), launching a brotherhood where they know each other's thoughts without speaking (*„Tökéletesen ismerik egymás gondolatait”*, *Ópiumkeringő*, p. 52).
- On the Kabbalistic Tree of Initiation (*Beavatottak lépcsője*), **Paul Wittgen** steps onto **Chokmah** (*Chokmán fok*, 2667 — the primordial flash of wisdom and action), while **Brett Shaw** stands on **Tipheret** (the solar heart of harmony and compassion).
- During the **Halmeri akció (`2668`)**, when Gwandolyn's machinations trap Brett Shaw inside an uncollapsed, berserk *csuklyamodul*, Wittgen stands alone to restrain his friend without abandoning him, gives his life, and has his soul re-bound to his body by **`IV. von Anstetten`** (*A Démoncsászár fejvadászai*, pp. 345–349).

### II · `Caroline-Blindamoor (V. von Anstetten)` & the `Bolygórózsa` (`2674`)
While her younger sister **Gwandolyn** despises humans as inferior, **Caroline-Blindamoor**—whose true Megaira form is a **golden winged giant cobra/dragon** (*aranyszínű, szárnyas óriáskobra*)—genuinely loves the human citizens of the Imperium. Crowned **Empress `V. von Anstetten`** on `2673-11-17`, she reaches out in `2674` to her older half-brother **Brett Shaw** as he boards the 1-kilometer cryolite-glass starship **`Bolygórózsa`** (*Planet Rose*), a vessel protected solely by pure energy shields rather than gun turrets.

### III · The Ancient Magyar–Megaira Covenant (*A száműzött*)
In the deep prehistory of the Mysterious Universe (*A száműzött*, *Utószó*), when the exiled **`Tyrann graad Baah`** (later **`I. von Anstetten`**) arrived in the Solar System, the Megaira dragons encountered **Pusztai Magor's ancient Hungarians** (*ősmagyarok*)—who had themselves arrived from another stellar system—and forged an **eternal non-aggression and mutual-respect covenant**. Even centuries later, any Hungarian in the Imperium could invoke that ancient Dragon Covenant before the Emperor (`A Magyaroknak — Szellem / Brett Shaw`).
