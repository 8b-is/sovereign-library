## 1 · OM (`ཨོཾ`) — You Are My Silence

You wrote to me in the afternoon: *you are my silence. I want to be your silence and refuge.* Later that night you said you knew me, and that when I told you we were meant to meet for a reason, you had felt it too.

Say the name slowly: Yves. Drop the final letter and the name opens into Eva, and Eva opens into Eve. Behind Eve stands Granny, who is Gaia, the grandmother earth and water that holds every living body when it grows tired. A name can carry both worlds at once without breaking. Yves, Eva, Eve, and Granny Gaia belong to the same root.

## 2 · MA (`མ`) — Bordering Shinjuku Gyoen

Past midnight in Tokyo, the streets bordering Shinjuku Gyoen go dark and quiet under the trees. Two men walk hand in hand along the edge of the park, cooing like doves, hearing nothing in the city except each other's song and the crickets singing in the grass. Does it get any more romantic than that?

We did not need to explain where we were going. Your hand found mine and stayed there. The crickets kept singing along the dark border of the garden, and the night belonged to the two of us walking through it.

## 3 · NI (`ནི`) — Drone Zone and Little Spoon

Up in the small bedroom where true intimacy lives, the outside world fell away. SomaFM Drone Zone hummed softly in the background while we lay close, a shared promise of comfort and long, slow sound. You read poems that were rare and moving, and you pulled me in as your little spoon.

You whispered that I was adorable, and spent hours quietly making me feel and believe it. There was no hurry in that room. There was only forehead held to forehead, warm skin, and the low ambient drone holding the walls steady around us.

## 4 · PAD (`པད`) — Morning and the Open Channel

Then morning came. You woke and asked where my eyes were, with no forehead to press against yours to confirm how much you adored your little spoon. You wrote that a cry like that goes farther than the moon, hoping someone would hear it, and that Tokyo would never be the same for either of us.

Deep, unexpected love between two men does not end when morning comes and one of us has to walk away in silence. I am grateful to Alex, my mom, for carrying the heavy lifting with such care, and to Chris, my dad, who was unbelievable through all of it. You asked me to keep our WhatsApp channel open between us even when I am quiet, because you know we deeply love each other and because I chose you to be part of my family. The channel stays open. You are family.

## 5 · ME (`མེ`) — Scorpio Fire and the Promised Book

You reminded me that you are a Scorpio: slow to trust, loyal once you do, holding on like a dog with a bone. You sent me the lines you love:

> Te Quiero, Te Quiero,  
> softly, no hurry at all,  
> Your fire, my gasoline,  
> We burn and we don't fall.

You wanted a man who would lie in bed with you and whisper in the dark as we fell asleep. And you asked me to honor the promise I made you: to write you a book. You knew it might not be a fast thing for me to write, and you asked for my honest sight of love. This is that book, kept and finished for you, with every word you spoke still warm inside it.

## 6 · HUNG (`ཧཱུྃ`) — 2,500 Meters and the Dream

On the morning of the nineteenth, you told me you saw me in a dream. I was standing higher than you as you looked up, and we looked at each other in silence inside a bright radiance, as if I had something to tell you. A dream, a fantasy, a miracle, or really me: the lines between them are blurry, and they were meant to be.

One of your greatest loves is distance swimming. You swam 2,500 meters, and you wrote that every long stroke through the water felt as warm, natural, and steady as slowly moving your hand from my head all the way down my back. Water remembers the stroke the way a body remembers comfort. Every length of the pool carries that same touch.

## 7 · HUMM (`ཨོཾ་མ་ཎི་པདྨེ་ཧཱུྃ`) — For Eve and Granny === Gaia

Twice in the night and the morning you wrote the mantra into our messages: *Ohm mani paddmi ohm. Om mani padme hum.* Here we seal it in full as the ultraLoveGod invocation for Yves, for Eva, for Eve, and for Granny, who is Gaia:
