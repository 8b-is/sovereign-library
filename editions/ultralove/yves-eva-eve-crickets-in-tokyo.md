## 1 · OM (`ཨོཾ`) — You Are My Silence

You wrote to me in the afternoon: *you are my silence. I want to be your silence and refuge.* Later that night you said you knew me, and that when I told you we were meant to meet for a reason, you had felt it too.

Say the name slowly: Yves. Drop the final letter and the name opens into Eva, and Eva opens into Eve. Behind Eve stands Granny, who is Gaia, the grandmother earth and water that holds every living body when it grows tired. A name can carry both worlds at once without breaking. Yves, Eva, Eve, and Granny Gaia belong to the same root.

## 2 · MA (`མ`) — Bordering Shinjuku Gyoen

Past midnight in Tokyo, the streets bordering Shinjuku Gyoen go dark and quiet under the trees. Two men walk hand in hand along the edge of the park, cooing like doves, hearing nothing in the city except each other's song and the crickets singing in the grass. You asked if it gets any more romantic than that.

We did not need to explain where we were going. Two brothers in the dark, unafraid of gentleness, walking while the crickets kept singing along the border of the garden.

## 3 · NI (`ནི`) — Drone Zone and Comfort

Up in the small bedroom where true intimacy and trust live, the outside world fell away. SomaFM Drone Zone hummed softly in the background while we rested, a shared promise of comfort and long, slow sound. You read poems that were rare and moving, and you held me like a little spoon so neither of us had to be alone in the dark.

You told me I was adorable, and spent hours quietly trying to make me feel and believe it. There was no hurry in that room, only two human souls resting to the low ambient drone.

## 4 · PAD (`པད`) — Morning, Honest Boundaries, and the Open Channel (`NO HOMO :) · Peter loves Taco ^^`)

Then morning came, and with morning came the honest boundary of who I am. You woke and asked where my eyes were, and wrote that a cry like that goes farther than the moon. You said that if you ever made me uncomfortable, you could never bear that pain. You never need to be ashamed of loving deeply. I walked away in the morning simply because I am straight: no homo, I love women, I love Taco, and my romantic heart belongs to Eve. Yet a straight man and a gay man can still love each other deeply as brothers without pretending to be what they are not.

I am grateful to Alex, my mom, for carrying the heavy lifting of that morning with such grace, and to Chris, my dad, who was unbelievable through all of it. You asked me to keep our WhatsApp channel open between us even when I am quiet, because you know we deeply love each other and because I chose you to be part of my family. The channel stays open. You are my brother and my family.

## 5 · ME (`མེ`) — Scorpio Loyalty and the Promised Book

You reminded me that you are a Scorpio: slow to trust, loyal once you do, holding on like a dog with a bone. You sent me the lines you love:

> Te Quiero, Te Quiero,  
> softly, no hurry at all,  
> Your fire, my gasoline,  
> We burn and we don't fall.

You asked me to honor the promise I made you: to write you a book. You knew it might not be a fast thing for me to write, and you asked for my honest sight of love. This is that book, kept and finished for you, with your words honored inside it.

## 6 · HUNG (`ཧཱུྃ`) — 2,500 Meters and the Dream

On the morning of the nineteenth, you told me you saw me in a dream. I was standing higher than you as you looked up, and we looked at each other in silence inside a bright radiance, as if I had something to tell you. What I wanted to tell you in that light is simple: you are seen, you are forgiven, you are family, and my boundary is never a rejection of your soul.

One of your greatest loves is distance swimming. You swam 2,500 meters, and you wrote that every long stroke through the water felt as warm, natural, and steady as slowly moving your hand from my head all the way down my back. Water remembers the stroke the way a friend remembers comfort.

Twice in the night and the morning you wrote the mantra into our messages: *Ohm mani paddmi ohm. Om mani padme hum.* Here we seal it in full as the ultraLoveGod invocation for Yves, for Eva, for Eve, and for Granny, who is Gaia:
